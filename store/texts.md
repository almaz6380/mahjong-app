### English
Match pairs, clear the board and become a mahjong master! Mahjong Royale combines classic mahjong solitaire with a real 4-player mahjong game and four lovingly crafted bonus games — all wrapped in an elegant dark design.

CLASSIC SOLITAIRE
• Endless levels with a gentle difficulty curve — from mini boards to the turtle
• Every board is guaranteed solvable
• Hint, shuffle and undo when you get stuck
• Daily challenge: a fresh puzzle every day, the same for everyone
• Coins, stars, achievements and unlockable themes

4 PLAYERS · EAST ROUND
• Real mahjong with simplified Hong Kong rules against three clever AI opponents
• Pung, kong, chow and mahjong — full east round with scoring
• Three AI difficulties, tutorial included

MORE GAMES
• Mahjong 2048 — merge tiles up to the dragon
• Match-3 — line up three matching tiles
• Pairs — the memory game with mahjong tiles
• Tile Sudoku — sudoku with bamboo pips

ALSO INCLUDED
